# Profit and Loss Questions

## 1. Cost price of article A

### Original question

Article A is marked up 20% and then sold at a discount. The loss on article B equals the discount offered on A. A and B are sold at a loss of 14% and 28% respectively. The cost price of B is Rs 675 more than A. Find the cost price of A.

### Calculation

Let the cost price of A be $a$. Its marked price is $1.2a$, and it is sold at a 14% loss, so its selling price is $0.86a$.

Discount on A:

$$
1.2a-0.86a=0.34a
$$

Let the cost price of B be $a+675$. Its loss is 28%:

$$
0.28(a+675)
$$

Equating the loss on B to the discount on A:

$$
0.28(a+675)=0.34a
$$

$$
0.28a+189=0.34a
$$

$$
0.06a=189
$$

$$
a=3150
$$

### Final answer

The cost price of A is **Rs 3,150**.

### Verification

Discount on A $=0.34\times3150=1071$. Loss on B, whose cost is Rs 3825, is $0.28\times3825=1071$.
