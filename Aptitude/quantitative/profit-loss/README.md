# Profit and Loss

## Formulae

$$
\text{Loss}=\text{CP}-\text{SP}
$$

$$
\text{Loss percentage}=\frac{\text{Loss}}{\text{CP}}\times100
$$

$$
\text{SP}=\text{CP}\left(1-\frac{\text{loss percentage}}{100}\right)
$$

Discount is calculated from marked price, while profit or loss is calculated from cost price.

## Method

Express each amount as a percentage of its own cost price. Equate the stated discount amount on one item to the stated loss amount on the other, then use the cost-price difference.

## Traps

- Discount percentage is based on marked price.
- Loss percentage is based on cost price.
- Keep the cost prices of different articles separate.
