# Mensuration Questions

## 1. Height of a cylinder from curved surface areas

### Original question

The base radius of a cone is 7 metres and its slant height is 55 metres. The curved surface of a cylinder is 20% less than that of the cone. The curved surface area of the cylinder is 352 square metres more than its base area. Find the height of the cylinder.

### Approach and calculation

Using $\pi=22/7$:

$$
\text{Cone CSA}=\pi rl=\frac{22}{7}\times7\times55=1210
$$

Cylinder CSA is 20% less:

$$
1210\times80\%=968
$$

Let the cylinder radius be $R$. Since its CSA is 352 more than its base area:

$$
\pi R^2=968-352=616
$$

$$
R^2=616\times\frac{7}{22}=196,\quad R=14
$$

Now:

$$
2\pi Rh=968
$$

$$
2\times\frac{22}{7}\times14\times h=968
$$

$$
h=11\text{ m}
$$

### Final answer

**11 metres.**

### Verification

Cylinder CSA $=88\times11=968$ and base area $=\frac{22}{7}\times14^2=616$; their difference is $352$.
