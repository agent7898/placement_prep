# Mensuration

## Definition and scope

Mensuration calculates perimeter, area, surface area, and volume of geometric figures.

## Formulae

Cone curved surface area:

$$
\text{CSA}=\pi r l
$$

Cylinder curved surface area:

$$
\text{CSA}=2\pi r h
$$

Cylinder base area:

$$
\text{Base area}=\pi r^2
$$

## Method

Calculate the cone's curved area, apply the stated percentage change, use the difference from the cylinder's base area to find its radius, then use the cylinder CSA formula to find its height.

## Traps

- Curved surface area excludes the base or bases.
- Keep metres and square metres consistent.
- Use the stated approximation for $\pi$ consistently.
