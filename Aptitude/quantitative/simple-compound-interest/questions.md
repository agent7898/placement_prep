# Simple and Compound Interest Questions

## 1. Determine the valid expressions

### Original question

Rihaan has Rs 5x. He invested 40% of it at 30% per annum simple interest for 5 years. He then invested half of the interest received, along with the remaining sum, at 20% per annum compound interest for 2 years, compounded annually, and received Rs 9720. The amount received at simple interest on Rs 5x for 2 years at 20% per annum will be:

I. Rs $(x+9000)$
II. Rs $(3x/5+9600)$
III. Rs $(3x+3000)$

### Calculation

Initial simple-interest investment is $40\%$ of $5x=2x$. Its interest for 5 years is:

$$
\frac{2x\times30\times5}{100}=3x
$$

The remaining sum is $3x$. Half the interest, $3x/2$, is invested with it:

$$
3x+\frac{3x}{2}=\frac{9x}{2}
$$

After two years at 20% compound interest:

$$
\frac{9x}{2}\times(1.2)^2=6.48x=9720
$$

$$
x=1500
$$

The amount from investing $5x$ at 20% simple interest for 2 years is:

$$
5x+\frac{5x\times20\times2}{100}=7x=10500
$$

Evaluate the options:

- I: $x+9000=1500+9000=10500$; true.
- II: $3x/5+9600=900+9600=10500$; true.
- III: $3x+3000=7500$; false.

### Final answer

**Statements I and II are correct.**
