# Simple and Compound Interest

## Formulae

Simple interest:

$$
\text{SI}=\frac{PRT}{100}
$$

Compound amount for annual compounding:

$$
A=P\left(1+\frac{R}{100}\right)^n
$$

## Method

Track the principal portions and interest separately. For compound interest, add the stated interest portion to the remaining sum before applying the compound-growth factor.

## Traps

- Simple interest is calculated on the original principal.
- Compound interest is calculated on the growing amount.
- Distinguish interest received from the final amount received.
