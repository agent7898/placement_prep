# Percentages

## Definition and scope

Percentage questions compare a part with a whole using a denominator of 100. They often combine subgroup percentages with total-population percentages.

## Formulae

$$
\text{Percentage}=\frac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}}\times100
$$

For a subgroup:

$$
\text{part in subgroup}=\text{subgroup size}\times\text{subgroup percentage}
$$

## Method

Convert every percentage into an actual count first. Subtract known subgroup counts from the total, then divide the remaining count by the remaining subgroup size.

## Traps

- The denominator for a female literacy percentage is the number of females, not the total population.
- Do not add percentages from groups with different sizes without weighting them.
