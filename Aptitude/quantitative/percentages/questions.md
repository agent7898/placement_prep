# Percentage Questions

## 1. Literacy among females

### Original question

Of the 1000 inhabitants of a town, 60% are males of whom 20% are literate. If 25% of all the inhabitants are literate, what percent of the females of the town are literate?

### Calculation

Males $=60\%$ of $1000=600$; females $=400$.

Literate males $=20\%$ of $600=120$.

Total literate inhabitants $=25\%$ of $1000=250$.

Literate females $=250-120=130$.

$$
\text{Female literacy percentage}=\frac{130}{400}\times100=32.5\%
$$

### Final answer

**32.5%**.

### Trap

The final percentage must use 400 females as its denominator.
