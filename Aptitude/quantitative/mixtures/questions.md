# Mixture Questions

## 1. Water added to change the milk-water ratio

### Original question

In 60 litres of a mixture, milk and water are in the ratio 7:3. How much water must be added so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 3:2?

### Calculation

Initial milk and water:

$$
\text{Milk}=60\times\frac{7}{10}=42\text{ L},\qquad \text{Water}=60\times\frac{3}{10}=18\text{ L}
$$

Let $x$ litres of water be added:

$$
\frac{42}{18+x}=\frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
84=54+3x
$$

$$
x=10
$$

### Final answer

**10 litres of water.**

### Verification

The final quantities are 42 L milk and 28 L water, giving $42:28=3:2$.
