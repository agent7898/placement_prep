# Mixtures

## Formulae

For two components, track the quantity of each component separately. The ratio is:

$$
\text{Ratio}=\frac{\text{quantity of first component}}{\text{quantity of second component}}
$$

Adding pure water changes only the water quantity.

## Method

Convert the initial ratio into actual quantities, let the added amount be $x$, and equate the new ratio to the target ratio.

## Traps

- Adding water does not change the amount of milk.
- Use the new total water amount in the denominator.
