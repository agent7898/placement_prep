# Average Questions

## 1. Winning streak needed to reach a 50% average

### Original question

In June a baseball team that played 60 games had won 30% of them. After a winning streak the team raised its average to 50%. How many games in a row must it have won?

### Tested concept

Changing average when consecutive wins are added to an existing win percentage.

### Approach

Let $x$ be the number of consecutive games won. The added wins increase both the number of wins and the total number of games, so the new denominator is $60 + x$.

### Step-by-step calculation

Initial wins:

$$
30\% \times 60 = \frac{30}{100} \times 60 = 18
$$

After winning $x$ games:

- Wins: $18 + x$
- Total games: $60 + x$
- Target average: $50\%$

Set up the average equation:

$$
\frac{18 + x}{60 + x} = \frac{50}{100} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
2(18 + x) = 60 + x
$$

$$
36 + 2x = 60 + x
$$

$$
x = 24
$$

### Final answer

The team must have won **24 games in a row**.

### Verification

After 24 consecutive wins:

$$
\text{Wins} = 18 + 24 = 42
$$

$$
\text{Games} = 60 + 24 = 84
$$

$$
\frac{42}{84} = \frac{1}{2} = 50\%
$$

### Mistake or trap to remember

The total number of games is not fixed at 60. The winning streak adds both $x$ wins and $x$ games to the calculation.

## 3. Average for the last four matches

### Original question

A cricketer's average for ten matches is 38.9 runs. If the average for the first six matches is 42, find the average for the last four.

### Tested concept

Finding a subgroup average from the total and another subgroup average.

### Step-by-step calculation

Total runs in ten matches:

$$
10\times38.9=389
$$

Runs in the first six matches:

$$
6\times42=252
$$

Runs in the last four matches:

$$
389-252=137
$$

Average for the last four:

$$
\frac{137}{4}=34.25
$$

### Final answer

The average for the last four matches is **34.25 runs**.

### Trap

Do not average 38.9 and 42 directly; the groups contain different numbers of matches.
