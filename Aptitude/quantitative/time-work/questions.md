# Time and Work Questions

## 1. Rishav, Arun, and Ankit complete a job

### Original question

Rishav can finish a work in 24 days. Arun is 20% less efficient than Rishav. Ankit takes 5 days less than Arun. Rishav and Arun start together and leave after finishing 90% of the work. Ankit finishes the rest alone. Find the total days taken.

### Calculation

Rishav's rate is $1/24$. Arun's rate is:

$$
80\%\times\frac{1}{24}=\frac{1}{30}
$$

Arun takes 30 days, so Ankit takes $30-5=25$ days and has rate $1/25$.

Together, Rishav and Arun work at:

$$
\frac{1}{24}+\frac{1}{30}=\frac{3}{40}
$$

Time to complete 90%:

$$
\frac{90}{100}\div\frac{3}{40}=12\text{ days}
$$

Remaining work is 10%. Ankit takes:

$$
\frac{10}{100}\div\frac{1}{25}=2.5\text{ days}
$$

### Final answer

$$
12+2.5=\boxed{14.5\text{ days}}
$$

### Trap

Efficiency and time are inversely related; a 20% lower rate gives Arun a 30-day completion time.

## 2. Additional workers join after eight days

### Original question

15 workers can build a wall in 24 days. After 8 days of work, 5 more workers join them. In how many days in total is the wall finished?

### Tested concept

Worker-days with a change in workforce.

### Step-by-step calculation

Total work:

$$
15\times24=360\text{ worker-days}
$$

Work completed in the first 8 days:

$$
15\times8=120\text{ worker-days}
$$

Remaining work:

$$
360-120=240\text{ worker-days}
$$

After 5 workers join, there are 20 workers. Remaining time:

$$
\frac{240}{20}=12\text{ days}
$$

Total time:

$$
8+12=20\text{ days}
$$

### Final answer

The wall is finished in **20 days in total**.

### Trap

The question asks for total elapsed time, so add the initial 8 days to the remaining 12 days.
