# Time and Work

## Definition and scope

Time-and-work problems use rates. A worker who completes a job in $d$ days has daily rate $1/d$ of the job.

## Formulae

$$
\text{Rate}=\frac{1}{\text{time}}
$$

$$
\text{Work}=\text{rate}\times\text{time}
$$

For workers with rates $r_1,r_2$:

$$
\text{Combined rate}=r_1+r_2
$$

## Method

Convert efficiency into rates, calculate the work completed in each phase, then calculate the remaining work using the workers present in that phase.

## Traps

- A 20% efficiency reduction means multiplying the rate by 80%, not increasing the time by 20%.
- Add elapsed times from separate phases.
- Express partial days when the arithmetic requires them.
