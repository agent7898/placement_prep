# Comparison and Ranking Questions

## 1. Heaviest iron article

### Original question

A blacksmith has five iron articles A, B, C, D and E, each of a different weight. A weighs twice as much as B. B weighs four and a half times as much as C. C weighs half as much as D. D weighs half as much as E. E weighs less than A but more than C. Which is the heaviest?

### Approach

Let $C=2$ units. Then:

$$
B=4.5C=9,
$$

$$
A=2B=18,
$$

$$
D=2C=4,
$$

$$
E=2D=8
$$

The condition $C<E<A$ is satisfied.

### Final answer

**Article A is the heaviest**, at 18 units in this scale.
