# Number Series Questions

## 1. Find the next term

### Original question

What comes next: 1, 7, 19, 39, ?

### Approach

Find successive differences:

$$
7-1=6,\quad19-7=12,\quad39-19=20
$$

The differences are $2\times3$, $3\times4$, and $4\times5$. The next difference is $5\times6=30$.

### Calculation

$$
39+30=69
$$

### Final answer

**69**.
