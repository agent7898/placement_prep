# Inequality Questions

## 1. Evaluate the conclusions

### Original question

Statements: T is less than M, M is less than or equal to Q. D is less than R. S is greater than or equal to M. T is less than A, A is less than D.

Conclusions: I. R is greater than T. II. S is greater than A. III. D is greater than M.

### Approach

Translate the useful chains:

$$
T<M\le Q,\qquad T<A<D<R,\qquad S\ge M
$$

### Evaluation

- I is true because $T<A<D<R$, so $R>T$.
- II does not necessarily follow. Although $S\ge M>T$, no fixed relation between $S$ and $A$ is given.
- III does not necessarily follow because the relation between $M$ and $D$ is not fixed.

### Final answer

**Only conclusion I follows.**
